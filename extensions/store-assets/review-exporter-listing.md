# ReviewExporter - Chrome Web Store Listing

## Title (max 45 chars)
ReviewExporter - Amazon Review to CSV

## Summary (max 132 chars)
Export Amazon product reviews to CSV with one click. Built-in AI analysis link for competitor research on ReviewMiner.

## Description

ReviewExporter scrapes product reviews from Amazon (and other e-commerce sites) and exports them to CSV in one click. Built for sellers and marketers who need to analyze customer feedback at scale.

### Features

- ONE CLICK EXPORT: extracts all visible reviews from the current page and saves as CSV
- AMAZON OPTIMIZED: targets Amazon review selectors for clean, structured data
- UNIVERSAL SCRAPER: also works on Shopee, Lazada, AliExpress, and other platforms
- REVIEWMINER INTEGRATION: one-click jump to AI-powered deep analysis to uncover pain points, sentiment trends, and competitive weaknesses
- INSTANT PREVIEW: see review count, product name, and preview snippets before exporting
- CSV FORMAT: exports standard CSV with title, body, rating, author, date, and verified badge columns

### How It Works

1. Navigate to any Amazon product page with reviews
2. Click the ReviewExporter icon
3. Reviews are automatically detected and previewed
4. Click "Export CSV" to download, or "Analyze with AI" to open ReviewMiner

### Who Is This For?

- Amazon / cross-border sellers researching competitors
- Product managers analyzing customer pain points
- Market researchers conducting VOC (Voice of Customer) studies
- Dropshippers validating product ideas before launching

### Privacy

All scraping happens locally in your browser. Review data is never sent to any server unless you explicitly choose to open ReviewMiner for AI analysis.
